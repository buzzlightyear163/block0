    How an EVEN launch works

    T–60s

    The window opens.

    Sixty seconds before the coin exists. There's no mint address yet, so there is nothing to snipe.

    Anyone commits.

    Put in SOL, up to the cap per wallet. A commit at second 1 counts exactly like a commit at second 59. Speed is worth nothing here.

    One bundle.

    At T–0 the coin is created and the whole pool buys in the same bundle. Block 0 is the pool.

    One price. Pro rata.

    your tokens = pool tokens × your SOL ÷ pool SOL

    Commit 0.5 SOL into an 18 SOL pool and you get 2.8% of what the pool bought, at the same price as every other wallet.

    Then it's a market.

    From block 1 the coin trades on its curve like any other. EVEN doesn't claim to stop anything after block 0, and neither should anyone else.

      FAQ

      What EVEN does, and what it doesn't.

      Example
      Is the EVEN pool live?

      Not yet. Pools are in a closed pilot. Gap Check and the Bundle Index are live today and read real data from Solana.

      Who holds the SOL in a pool?

      Not a wallet anyone on the team controls. Pools are planned on Meteora's Alpha Vault, an audited on-chain program that holds deposits and buys for the pool in pro-rata mode.

      Can a bot just use many wallets?

      Yes, and it gets a bigger share for it, never a better price. Pilot pools add a sign-up list with a cap per wallet to make that expensive.

      What happens after block 0?

      The coin trades on its curve like any other. EVEN doesn't claim sniper protection after the first block.

      Won't the whole pool sell at once?

      Pilot pools vest over a few minutes, so everyone in the pool can't sell in the same second.

      How does Gap Check work?

      It finds the coin's first slot through Solana's public RPC, reads every transaction in it and adds up what each wallet bought. The price now comes from the bonding curve or DexScreener. It works best on coins under a day old. On very busy coins it stops and says so instead of guessing.

      What counts toward the Bundle Index?

      A sampled pump.fun launch counts when the wallets that bought in its create slot took 20% of supply or more. The dev's own buy is included, because it lands in the same block.

      Is the gap a trading signal?

      No. It's a number about who got in first, not advice to buy or sell. Nothing on this site is financial advice.

      Will EVEN DM me?

      Never. EVEN will never ask for your keys, your seed phrase or a deposit in a DM.
